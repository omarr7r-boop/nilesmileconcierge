Treatment Timeline

How Many Visits Do You Need?

Every treatment has a different timeline. Some can be completed in a single trip — others require two visits with a healing gap between them. Here is exactly what to expect.

Treatment Package Visits Required First Visit Healing Gap Second Visit Total Duration
Signature Zirconia Smile 1 visit 7–8 days — — 7–8 days
Prime Bite Full Restoration 2 visits 7–8 days 3–6 months 5–7 days 5–7 months
Forever Young Full-Arch 2 visits 7–10 days 3–6 months 5–7 days 5–7 months
Implant Rescue Program 2–3 visits 7–14 days 4–6 months 5–7 days 6–9 months
Complete Smile Alignment 2 visits 3–5 days 6–12 months (remote) 3–5 days 6–12 months

Why two visits? Dental implants require a biological healing phase called osseointegration — the implant fuses with your jawbone over 3–6 months. The final prosthesis cannot be placed until this healing is complete. This is not optional; it is how implant dentistry works worldwide.

Important: The healing gap is not time spent in Egypt — you return home during this period and come back for the second visit. Final visit dates are confirmed in your written treatment plan.

Is Egypt safe for medical travel?+
Yes. Egypt is one of the world's top tourism destinations, welcoming over 15 million visitors annually and targeting further growth in 2025–2026. Our partner clinics are located in secure, upscale districts of Cairo. We provide private VIP transportation throughout your stay and a dedicated coordinator accompanies you to all clinic visits.
Do I need a visa to enter Egypt?+

US, Canadian, UK, EU, Australian, and New Zealand passport holders can obtain a visa on arrival at Egyptian airports for $25 USD (valid 30 days). We provide full documentation to support your entry, including an invitation letter and treatment confirmation.

Note: Only use the official government portal. Third-party visa sites charge additional fees. We provide full documentation support regardless of which method you choose.

Are your dentists licensed and certified?+
Yes. All treating dentists are licensed by the Egyptian Dental Syndicate and hold postgraduate qualifications from top institutions (Cairo, Alexandria, Ain Shams). Credentials are shared with you before booking.
What happens if I have a problem after returning home?+
You receive a written aftercare protocol, scheduled telehealth follow-ups (1 week, 1 month, 3 months), and a documented 2-tier implant warranty. We coordinate with a licensed local dentist in your country when needed.
Can I combine treatment with a family vacation?+
Absolutely. Many patients bring their families. While you are in treatment or recovery, your family can enjoy curated tours of the Pyramids, Nile cruises, Red Sea resorts, and the Egyptian Museum. We arrange family-friendly accommodation.
Does my insurance cover treatment in Egypt?+

Short answer: In the vast majority of cases, US, Canadian, and UK dental insurance does not cover treatment performed in Egypt. Treat all pricing as self-pay unless you have written confirmation from your insurer stating otherwise.

Why? Most dental insurance plans operate within a limited geographic network. Treatment abroad is typically classified as out-of-network or excluded entirely. Some PPO plans may reimburse a small portion, but this is rare and requires pre-authorization.

How we help: We provide you with:

  • Itemized invoices with ADA-compatible procedure codes
  • Detailed treatment records and imaging
  • Letters of medical necessity where applicable

You can submit these to your insurer for possible partial reimbursement. We do not guarantee coverage.

Recommendation: Consider a standalone medical travel insurance policy that specifically covers dental treatment abroad. This is different from standard travel insurance and typically costs $50–$150 for the trip.

What are the risks of dental implants?+
Implant success rates exceed 94.6% in systematic reviews. The most common complication is peri-implantitis (inflammation around the implant), affecting approximately 9.6% of implants. Our prevention protocol includes CBCT-guided planning, premium implant surfaces, and 6-month maintenance intervals. Full written disclosure of risks is provided before treatment.

It depends on the treatment:

1 visit (7–8 days): Signature Zirconia Smile — crowns only, no implants.

2 visits (5–7 months total): Prime Bite, Forever Young, Complete Smile Alignment.

2–3 visits (6–9 months total): Implant Rescue Program — complex cases with bone grafting.

What is the total time from first contact to final smile?+

A typical full journey looks like this:

  • Week 0: Free virtual consultation + written treatment plan
  • Weeks 1–4: Booking, flights, and travel logistics
  • Visit 1: Surgery + temporary prosthesis (7–10 days in Egypt)
  • Months 1–6: Healing at home + remote follow-ups
  • Visit 2: Final prosthesis fitting (5–7 days in Egypt)

Total: 5–7 months from first contact to final result. Only 12–17 days of that is spent in Egypt.

Can I complete everything in one visit?+

For crowns and veneers only: Yes. Signature Zirconia Smile can be completed in a single 7–8 day visit because there are no implants — just precision crowns on existing teeth.

For implants: No — and this is true worldwide. Dental implants require a biological healing phase (osseointegration) of 3–6 months before the final prosthesis can be placed. No ethical dentist — in Egypt, the US, or anywhere — will place a permanent implant-supported prosthesis on the same day as surgery.

If a clinic offers "implants in one visit," ask exactly what they mean. Some use same-day temporary bridges, which is legitimate — but the final permanent restoration still requires a second visit after healing.

If your treatment requires two visits, here is what happens:

  • You return home after the first visit with a fixed temporary prosthesis and a complete aftercare protocol.
  • Remote follow-ups continue via WhatsApp and telehealth at 1 week, 1 month, and 3 months.
  • Your second visit is scheduled in advance — typically 4–6 months after the first — and confirmed in writing in your original treatment plan.
  • Pricing for the second visit is included in your original quote. You do not pay twice for the same treatment.
  • Travel add-on costs apply per visit — meaning if you choose an add-on package, it applies to each trip separately. We give you a bundled estimate at the start.

Important: The second visit is not optional — it is required to place the final permanent prosthesis after healing. Final results cannot be achieved in a single visit for implant cases.

What anesthesia options are available?+

We offer three levels of anesthesia, selected based on your procedure complexity and personal preference:

1. Local Anesthesia — Standard for single implants, crowns, and minor procedures. You remain fully awake and comfortable, with no pain sensation in the treated area.

2. Local Anesthesia + Oral Sedation — For patients with dental anxiety. Oral sedatives (like Valium or Halcion) are taken 30–60 minutes before the procedure. You remain conscious but deeply relaxed.

3. IV Sedation (Conscious Sedation) — Administered by a licensed anesthesiologist. You're in a dream-like state, relaxed and comfortable, with no memory of the procedure. Ideal for full-arch (All-on-4 / All-on-6) surgeries.

Important: General anesthesia is not typically required for dental implant procedures. All anesthesia is administered or supervised by licensed medical professionals in our JCI-aligned partner clinics.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?+

Good candidates for dental implants generally meet these criteria:

  • Adults over 18 (jawbone must be fully developed)
  • Adequate bone density — or willing to undergo bone grafting
  • Healthy gum tissue with no active periodontal disease
  • No uncontrolled chronic conditions (diabetes, heart disease)
  • Non-smoker, or willing to quit during healing
  • Committed to good oral hygiene and regular follow-ups
  • Able to travel to Cairo for treatment (7–14 days minimum)

Not suitable for: Patients on IV bisphosphonates for cancer, those with recent heart attack (within 6 months), active cancer treatment, uncontrolled diabetes, or severe immune disorders.

What medications will I need after surgery?+

Your dentist will prescribe a personalized medication plan. Typical protocols include:

Antibiotics: Amoxicillin (or Clindamycin for penicillin-allergic patients) — usually 5–7 days post-op to prevent infection.

Pain relief: Ibuprofen 400mg (every 6–8 hours) and/or Paracetamol 500mg. Avoid aspirin, which can increase bleeding.

Anti-inflammatory: Prescribed for swelling control during the first 3–5 days.

Antiseptic mouthwash: Chlorhexidine 0.12% — starting 24 hours after surgery.

Important: Never exceed the maximum daily dose of Paracetamol (4,000 mg per day for adults). Do not combine multiple medications containing Paracetamol without medical advice.

What does the aftercare protocol include?+

You receive a complete written aftercare protocol before departure, covering:

Day 1–3: Rest, ice packs (20 min on / 20 min off), soft cold foods, avoid rinsing forcefully, no smoking, no straws.

Day 4–7: Gentle warm salt water rinses, soft foods (yogurt, soup, mashed potatoes), start prescribed antibiotics and mouthwash.

Week 2–4: Return to most normal activities, gradually introduce semi-solid foods, avoid hard/crunchy items, hygiene checks via telehealth.

Long-term: Brush twice daily with a soft brush, use interdental brushes and water flossers, attend 6-month check-ups (in Cairo or with a local dentist we coordinate with).

You'll also receive emergency contact information and 24/7 WhatsApp access to your clinical coordinator.

Implant Warranty — Full Terms

Structure of Coverage: Implants carry a two-tier warranty:
