What you are really afraid of

Three fears. We built Nile Smile around all of them.

“What if it doesn’t work?”

You have already imagined the negative test. We will not pretend we can promise you a baby — no honest clinic can. What we can promise is that you will never meet a bad result alone, uninformed, or rushed into the next cycle before you are ready.

“What if we cannot afford another round?”

IVF is one of the few purchases where the final bill can arrive as a shock. Our pricing is written before you start, itemised line by line, and fixed for your cycle. If something is not included, you will know before you commit — not after.

“What if I am just a number?”

In a standard clinic, your scan may be with whoever is on duty. Here, one consultant owns your protocol, one coordinator owns your logistics, and one nurse owns your emotional care. The same faces, from first call to final review.

The Nile Smile model

A concierge practice, not an assembly line.

Most international fertility journeys begin with a price list and end with a different doctor at every appointment. You repeat your history to strangers. You chase your own results. You learn what a monitoring scan is from a forum at two in the morning.

Nile Smile was designed backwards from that experience. We kept the medicine — consultant-led IVF, ICSI and genetic testing in an accredited Cairo facility — and rebuilt everything around it: continuity, clarity, and emotional care that is scheduled into the cycle rather than offered as an afterthought.

You are not buying a procedure. You are buying a plan that someone is accountable for.

  • One consultant, from your first protocol to your pregnancy test.
  • A written, itemised quote before any payment is taken.
  • A named coordinator reachable on WhatsApp, not a call centre queue.
  • Psychological support included in the package — never upsold as an extra.

The person behind the difference

Your dedicated psychological fertility nurse.

Every Nile Smile patient is assigned one nurse whose only job is to keep you emotionally steady while the medicine does its work.

She is not a call centre. She is not a chatbot with a script. She is a fertility nurse trained in emotional support who learns your history, your triggers, and the way you prefer to be spoken to — then stays with you for the whole cycle.

  • Needle phobia is treated as a real clinical problem, with a written plan — not a pep talk.
  • The two-week wait has structure: scheduled check-ins, symptom guidance, and a person to call when the internet has frightened you.
  • A difficult result is delivered by someone who already knows your story, with time to sit with it.
