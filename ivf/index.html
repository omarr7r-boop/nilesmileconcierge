Ovarian reserve & personalised stimulation

We begin with the numbers that actually predict your response: AMH (anti-Müllerian hormone) and antral follicle count (AFC) on day 2–3, combined with your age, BMI and prior cycle history. Those results — not a fixed protocol — choose your stimulation strategy.

  • Antagonist protocol — flexible, shorter, and the standard choice for most cycles.
  • Long agonist protocol — for specific endometriosis, adenomyosis or synchronisation needs.
  • DuoStim — two stimulations within a single menstrual cycle for urgent or low-reserve cases.
  • Mild / Mini-IVF — gentler, lower-dose stimulation for low responders or where safety of OHSS is a concern.

Where indicated, we also perform diagnostic and operative hysteroscopy to correct polyps, adhesions or a septum before transfer, and endometrial receptivity testing (ERA, EMMA, ALICE) to time the window of implantation precisely. Luteal-phase support is then precision-matched to your own hormone profile rather than given as a fixed dose.

Internal medicine & reproductive endocrinology

An embryo can be perfect and still fail to implant in a body that is not physiologically ready. Before transfer we screen and stabilise the systems that quietly decide outcomes.

  • Thyroid function — TSH and anti-TPO antibodies, with treatment to a preconception target range.
  • Insulin resistance & PCOS — metabolic balancing, which improves egg quality and reduces miscarriage risk.
  • Thrombophilia & autoimmune screening — inherited and acquired clotting risk, plus autoimmune markers, where your history warrants it.
  • Vitamin D & micronutrients — corrected well before stimulation, not during the two-week wait.
  • Chronic-condition stabilisation — diabetes, hypertension and thyroid disease brought under control with your existing specialists.

This is why our pre-treatment medical assessment runs in parallel with the fertility plan. A transfer scheduled into an optimised body has a materially better chance than one scheduled into an inflamed one.

Advanced IVF, ICSI & embryology science

Your embryos are grown in an ISO-standard laboratory under continuous monitoring, with every decision explained in language you can actually use.

  • High-magnification ICSI, PICSI and IMSI — selecting sperm by morphology and physiological maturity rather than movement alone.
  • Time-lapse EmbryoScope incubators — uninterrupted culture with continuous imaging, so embryos are assessed without being disturbed.
  • Day-5 / Day-6 blastocyst culture — extended culture to select the embryos most likely to implant.
  • PGT-A and PGT-M — chromosomal screening and single-gene disorder testing, where clinically indicated.
  • Laser-assisted hatching — for selected embryos where the shell is unusually thick.
  • Ultra-rapid vitrification — fast, high-survival freezing for any embryos you choose to preserve.

Male-factor infertility is treated as a first-class diagnosis here, not a footnote. PICSI and IMSI exist precisely for the cycles where standard ICSI has previously under-performed.

Clinical protocols & success

What are realistic success rates, and how much does age really matter?

Success depends far more on age and ovarian reserve than on the clinic. As a general guide for a single transfer of a genetically normal Day-5 blastocyst, live-birth rates are roughly 50–60% under 35, 40–50% at 35–37, 30–40% at 38–40, and progressively lower above 40. With PGT-A screening and a euploid embryo, outcomes per transfer improve — but the number of usable embryos available to you falls with age.

We give you your own personalised estimate after your AMH, AFC and semen analysis, not a brochure average.

Which stimulation protocols do you use, and why does guideline alignment matter?

We use the full evidence-based range: antagonist, long agonist, DuoStim (two stimulations in one cycle) and mild/Mini-IVF for low responders. Your protocol is chosen from your AMH, AFC, age, BMI and prior response — not a fixed clinic template.

Our protocols are aligned with ASRM (US), HFEA guidance (UK) and RANZCOG (Australia/New Zealand), so your care follows the same evidence base you would receive at home.

I've been told my BMI or age rules me out. Will you refuse me too?

We do not apply blanket BMI or age cut-offs. We assess you individually, explain honestly what risks and realistic expectations apply at your BMI or age, and where necessary build a safer, staged plan. No clinic should turn you away at a number without a conversation — and we will also tell you plainly if a different path is medically wiser.

What is AMH and antral follicle count testing, and how does it change my plan?

AMH (anti-Müllerian hormone) is a blood test that estimates your ovarian reserve; antral follicle count (AFC) is an ultrasound count of the small follicles available that cycle. Together they predict how you will respond to stimulation, guide your starting dose, and tell us whether a standard, mild or DuoStim approach gives you the best chance.

Do you offer PGT-A genetic screening, and does it improve my odds?

Yes. PGT-A screens embryos for chromosomal abnormalities, and PGT-M tests for specific single-gene disorders. PGT-A can raise the live-birth rate per transfer and lower miscarriage risk in suitable cases, but it does not create embryos — if you have few, it may leave you with nothing to transfer. We only recommend it where your age, history and embryo numbers make it genuinely worthwhile.

Is male-factor infertility treated properly, or as an afterthought?

It is a first-class diagnosis here. We use high-magnification ICSI, PICSI (physiological sperm selection) and IMSI (morphology-based selection) where standard ICSI has previously under-performed, alongside a full andrology work-up. Male-factor stigma is addressed directly with psychological support, not ignored.

Timeline & travel

How much time do I actually need to be in Cairo?

Most patients need roughly 10–14 days in Cairo for the stimulation tail-end, egg retrieval and, in a fresh transfer cycle, the transfer itself. A frozen-transfer cycle needs fewer days. Where your local clinic is willing, the early monitoring scans of stimulation can be done at home and shared with us by secure transfer, shortening your stay.

Can I do any of the cycle remotely from the US, UK, Canada or Australia?

Often, yes. Baseline bloods and scans, and early stimulation monitoring, can frequently be done locally and reviewed by your Nile Smile consultant. Retrieval and transfer must happen in Cairo. We tell you precisely which visits can be remote and which cannot — before you book flights.

What is the full timeline, from first call to pregnancy test?

A typical fresh cycle runs around 6–8 weeks from first consultation to pregnancy test: a remote consultation and baseline testing in week 1, stimulation for 8–12 days, retrieval, then Day-5/6 blastocyst development and transfer. Frozen cycles are timed to your cycle and usually complete within a similar window, with fewer days in Cairo.

How does travel and accommodation coordination work?

Your coordinator handles the practical side: appointment scheduling, recommended accommodation near the clinic, airport transfers, and direct-flight guidance from major US, UK, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand hubs. Cairo is served by direct flights from London, New York, Toronto and other major cities, and entry visas are typically straightforward for these nationals.

Money & transparency

What is the total cost, and what is genuinely excluded?

Our packages are all-inclusive and quoted before you commit, covering consultation, medication, monitoring, retrieval, laboratory work, ICSI and — depending on the package — genetic screening and a year of embryo freezing. Genuinely excluded items are few and always stated in writing: travel, accommodation, and any additional cycle you choose to undertake. There are no hidden medication, lab or freezing fees added later.

Which currencies do you quote in, and how do I pay?

We quote transparently in USD, GBP, CAD and AUD so you can compare directly with prices at home. Payment is staged against clear milestones and confirmed in writing, with no FX surprises buried in the small print.

How does the pricing compare with a cycle at home?

A single private cycle in the US or Canada commonly runs US$25,000–$38,000+ once medication, genetic testing and freezing are added; in the UK fully private IVF typically starts around £5,000–£8,000 before add-ons. Nile Smile's all-inclusive packages are positioned to deliver the same clinical scope for substantially less, with the guarantee that the quoted figure is the figure.

Emotional care, privacy & getting started

How does the dedicated psychological fertility nurse support us?

One nurse is assigned to your cycle and stays with you throughout. She runs scheduled check-ins during stimulation, coaches you through injections or needle phobia, provides structure during the two-week wait, and schedules dedicated sessions for your partner. She is the person you call when the internet has frightened you at midnight — and the person who delivers difficult news with the time it deserves.

My partner feels sidelined. How do you include him?

By design, not by accident. He has his own sessions covering performance pressure, male-factor stigma and guilt, and is included in decisions rather than informed afterwards. Many of our patients tell us this is the first time a clinic asked how he was coping.

How do you protect our privacy?

Your case is handled privately and stored securely, and no data leaves your browser on this website until you choose to send it via WhatsApp on the consultation form. Discreet scheduling, private suites and limited staff access to your file are standard. We can provide a written privacy charter on request.

What happens if the cycle fails?

We review every failed cycle with you properly — protocol, embryo quality, endometrial factors and timing — before deciding what changes next. Your nurse stays with you through the result, and your consultant gives you an honest, written plan for any further cycle rather than a sales call. You are never left to interpret a negative test alone.

How do I start, and what do you need from me?

Begin with the consultation form or a WhatsApp message. Share your age, any prior cycles, and your most recent AMH, AFC or semen analysis if you have them. A coordinator will arrange a private consultation with the consultant in your time zone — no deposit, no obligation, and an honest answer about whether we are the right clinic for you.
