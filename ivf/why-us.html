What standard IVF clinics get wrong

Four fears. Four deliberate answers.

These are not marketing problems. They are structural problems — and they are fixable if a clinic is willing to lose a little margin to fix them.

01

Hidden fees

The usual story

A headline price that excludes medication, anaesthesia, lab fees, embryo freezing and follow-up. You discover the real number one payment at a time, usually when you are too invested to walk away.

At Nile Smile

An itemised quote before you commit, locked for your cycle, with inclusions and exclusions in writing. If it is not on the page, you will not be billed for it without a new conversation and your explicit approval.

02

Assembly-line medicine

The usual story

Whoever is on duty performs your scan. Your protocol is adjusted by a registrar you have never met. Continuity is a word in the brochure, not a fact in the corridor.

At Nile Smile

One lead consultant owns your diagnosis, your protocol and every clinical decision. You know their name before you book, and they review every result — including the ones that arrive while they are away.

03

Cold treatment

The usual story

You are a file number. Questions are rushed. Bad news arrives through a portal notification, and the next appointment is in three weeks.

At Nile Smile

A named coordinator and a dedicated nurse. Results are delivered by a person who knows your history, with time to talk, and a written plan for what happens next.

04

Zero psychological support

The usual story

A leaflet about stress and a suggestion to “try to relax”. Emotional care is outsourced to a forum, a friend, or nobody at all.

At Nile Smile

Scheduled emotional care built into the cycle: needle-phobia plans, two-week-wait structure, results support and a debrief after a loss. Included, not upsold.

Step by step

How each fear is removed, in order.

Not with reassurance. With a process you can inspect before you pay anything.

  1. 1

    The first conversation is an assessment, not a sale

    We ask about your history, your previous cycles, your budget and your fears. If we cannot help you, we tell you — and explain where else to go. A clinic that will take anyone is not selecting for your outcome.

  2. 2

    You receive a written plan and a fixed quote

    Diagnosis, protocol, medication, lab work, coordination and emotional support — itemised. You approve it before anything begins. Optional treatments are labelled optional, with the consequences of declining explained.

  3. 3

    You meet your team before you pay

    Names, roles and contact routes. You will know who calls with results, who answers the night before retrieval, and who to message when a question cannot wait until Monday.

  4. 4

    Your cycle is coordinated around your life

    Travel, accommodation, appointment times and medication delivery planned backwards from your dates — not the clinic’s convenience. If a flight changes, the plan changes with it.

  5. 5

    Your emotional care is scheduled, not promised

    Intake, injection coaching, two-week-wait check-ins and a post-result debrief are appointments in your calendar. Not phrases in a brochure. Not something you have to ask for twice.

The conversation most clinics avoid

Financial stress, addressed honestly.

IVF is expensive, and pretending otherwise insults you. Many families are making decisions with money that was meant for a home, a business, or another child’s education. That pressure is real, and it changes how you read every conversation with a clinic.

We will never use that pressure against you. There is no commission-driven upsell, no “while you are here” add-on, and no unexplained line item. If a treatment is optional, we will say it is optional and tell you what happens if you decline.

If the numbers do not work for you, we will tell you what a smaller, staged plan could look like — or that now is not the right time. A clinic that needs your money more than it needs your trust will not do that. We will.

What your quote will always show

  • Medication, with brand and dose where possible.
  • Anaesthesia and theatre fees.
  • Embryology, ICSI and laboratory charges.
  • Embryo freezing and the first year of storage.
  • Coordinator, nursing and psychological support.
  • A clear list of what is not included.

No payment is taken before you have read and approved the full quote. If the final invoice differs from the quote without your written agreement, we will honour the quote.

Medical honesty

We will not promise you a baby.

Any clinic that guarantees success is selling you something other than medicine. IVF outcomes depend on age, diagnosis, ovarian reserve, sperm quality, embryo genetics and factors nobody fully understands. What we can promise is honesty about your specific odds, in writing, with the reasoning behind them.

We will show you our clinic’s published outcomes and explain what they do and do not mean. We will tell you when a treatment is unlikely to help. We will not enrol you in a cycle to improve a statistic, and we will not keep you in treatment longer than is clinically justified.

Our care is Sharia-compliant by design. Consent, family involvement and the boundaries of treatment are discussed openly, respectfully and without assumption — including the questions you may feel awkward asking a stranger.

Nile Smile clinical philosophy

A two-way agreement

What we ask of you.

Good fertility care is a partnership, not a transaction. These five commitments make the biggest difference to your experience — and to your odds of a calm, well-informed cycle.

  • Tell us the truth about your history, even the parts that feel embarrassing.
  • Ask the question you think is stupid. It is usually the most important one.
  • Bring your partner into the process, if you have one. IVF is not a solo sport.
  • Tell us when the plan stops working for your life. We will adjust it.
  • Give yourself permission to pause. A delay is not a failure.
