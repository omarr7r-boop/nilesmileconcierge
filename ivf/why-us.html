Why come to Cairo · Why Nile Smile

Why patients from the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand choose Cairo.

For medical travellers, the hardest part of IVF is rarely the medicine. It is the 12–24 month public waitlist, the US$25,000–$38,000+ out-of-pocket cycle that can still grow after you commit, the rigid BMI and age gatekeeping, and the rushed five-minute appointment with a clinician who has already moved on. Nile Smile is a private, consultant-led practice in Cairo built as the opposite of all four — without hiding what a journey abroad genuinely asks of you.

Standards you can check

Protocols aligned with the guidelines you already trust.

International patients ask a fair question: if the treatment is in Cairo, is the medicine held to the standards I know at home? Our clinical protocols are aligned with the American Society for Reproductive Medicine (ASRM), the UK Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA) guidance, and the Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (RANZCOG) — the same evidence base your own doctors work from. That alignment is not a slogan; it decides which stimulation protocol you are offered, how your embryos are cultured, what we screen for, and what we refuse to do.

You are entitled to inspect these commitments before you pay anything. Below is what you can hold us to, in writing.

ISO-standard embryology lab

Your embryos are grown and handled in an ISO-standard laboratory with controlled air quality, calibrated equipment and documented daily practice.

One named consultant

Your protocol, your scans and your decisions stay with one clinician whose name you know before you book — no rotating duty roster.

Written, all-inclusive pricing

Medication, monitoring, lab work and freezing quoted up front in USD, GBP, CAD or AUD — so you can compare directly with prices at home.

No BMI or age gatekeeping

We assess you individually and say honestly what is realistic — we do not turn you away at a number without a conversation.

Documented quality control

Every gamete and embryo is traceable from collection to storage, with logged quality-control checks at each step of the laboratory pathway.

What we will never do

Five promises we can keep precisely because they are refusals.

Honesty in fertility care is easier to describe by what we decline than by what we claim. These are not aspirations; they are the lines that decide how we run every single cycle, and you are welcome to test us against them at any point.

If we ever break one of these, we want you to say so — in writing, to the clinic, not quietly to yourself. That is the standard we are holding ourselves to.

Nile Smile refuses to

  • Promise you a baby, or quote a guaranteed success rate.
  • Hide fees, or add line items you did not agree to in writing.
  • Rotate your clinician, so you repeat your story to strangers.
  • Upsell psychological support as an optional extra.
  • Pressure you toward another cycle after a failed one.

When a clinic says no to these five, everything it says yes to means more.

Zero waitlists means no queue to join, no 12–24 month deferral, and no cut-off that moves while you wait. One dedicated fertility nurse is assigned to your cycle, from first injection to final debrief. A maximum 24-hour reply time means a human being in your time zone, not an out-of-hours call centre. And four clinical pillars — gynaecology, endocrinology, embryology and psychology — work from one shared record rather than four departments passing you between them.

Outcomes and honesty

We will tell you the odds, not sell you the dream.

Any clinic that guarantees success is selling you something other than medicine. IVF outcomes depend on age, ovarian reserve, sperm quality, embryo genetics and factors nobody fully understands. What we can promise is honesty about your specific odds, in writing, with the reasoning behind them.

We will show you our clinic's published outcomes and explain what they do and do not mean. We will tell you when a treatment is unlikely to help. We will not enrol you in a cycle to improve a statistic, and we will not keep you in treatment longer than is clinically justified.

For international patients, that honesty extends to the logistics: we tell you precisely which monitoring visits can be done at home and which must happen in Cairo, before you book a single flight. If we are not the right clinic for you, we will say so — and point you toward where you are more likely to be helped.

No pressure. No obligation.
