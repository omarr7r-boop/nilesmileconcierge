Before you fill anything in

You are allowed to ask without committing.

Most people who contact a fertility clinic are not ready to book. They are trying to understand whether their situation is treatable, whether they can afford it, and whether the clinic will treat them like a person. Those are the right questions, and none of them require you to become a patient.

This first conversation is an assessment, not a transaction. We will ask about your history, your previous cycles, your budget and your fears. If we are not the right clinic for you — because of your diagnosis, your timeline or your location — we will say so and, where we can, point you somewhere better suited.

You do not need to have your medical records ready. You do not need to know which treatment you want. You do not even need to be sure you want treatment at all.

What we will never do

  • Call you repeatedly after you ask us not to.
  • Share your enquiry with a third party or a marketing list.
  • Quote a price on the call and change it after you commit.
  • Tell you that you must decide today to secure a place.
  • Promise a baby, a success rate or an outcome we cannot guarantee.

If any of this happens, raise it directly with the clinical lead. Not a generic inbox.

After you press send

Four steps. No surprises.

  1. 1

    A human reads it

    Usually within 24 hours on working days. If your message raises something urgent, we will say so and tell you what to do next rather than waiting for a scheduled call.

  2. 2

    A private 30-minute conversation

    By video or phone, at a time that suits you. You can bring your partner, a friend, or nobody at all. You can also ask to speak to a female coordinator.

  3. 3

    A written plan and a fixed quote

    What we would investigate, what treatment we would recommend, what it would cost and what is not included. In writing, so you can read it without anyone watching your face.

  4. 4

    You decide in your own time

    There is no deadline and no follow-up campaign. If you want a second opinion elsewhere, take one. If you decide to wait a year, tell us — the door stays open.

Discretion by default

Nobody needs to know you asked.

Fertility is private, and in many families it is complicated. You may not want your employer, your extended family or your community to know that you are exploring treatment. That is your decision, and we protect it.

You can begin with a first name or an alias. We will not confirm to anyone that you are a patient. Clinic visits are arranged through a private entrance where available, and your records are held under a confidential reference rather than your full name on a visible file.

If a relative calls asking whether you have an appointment, we will neither confirm nor deny it. If you want your partner involved, we will include them — with your written consent, and only to the extent you choose.

Your privacy controls

  • An alias is enough to start the conversation.
  • No marketing use of your story without separate, specific consent.
  • No sharing with third parties, including family, without your written agreement.
  • Private arrival and departure where the facility allows it.
  • Clinical notes stored to standard medical record-keeping requirements.
  • A direct route to the clinical lead if you believe your privacy has been breached.

Your enquiry is not added to a mailing list. There is no newsletter to unsubscribe from.
