    Everything international patients ask

    The questions that decide whether you trust a clinic.

    These are the answers we give on the call, written down so you can read them slowly, at home, without anyone watching your face.

    Clinical protocols & success

    What are realistic success rates, and how much does age really matter?

    Success depends far more on your age and ovarian reserve than on the clinic, and anyone who quotes you a single headline number is selling, not treating. As a general guide for a single transfer of a genetically normal Day-5 blastocyst, live-birth rates sit at roughly 50–60% under 35, 40–50% at 35–37, 30–40% at 38–40, and fall progressively above 40. With PGT-A screening of a chromosomally normal embryo, the rate per transfer improves — but the number of usable embryos available to you falls with age.

    We do not give you a brochure average. After your AMH, antral follicle count and semen analysis, your consultant gives you a personalised estimate for your own biology, and explains honestly what a realistic plan looks like.

    Which stimulation protocols do you use, and how are they chosen?

    We use the full evidence-based range: the antagonist protocol (flexible and the standard choice for most cycles), the long agonist protocol (for specific endometriosis, adenomyosis or synchronisation needs), DuoStim (two stimulations within a single menstrual cycle for urgent or low-reserve cases), and mild / Mini-IVF for low responders or where the risk of OHSS makes gentler stimulation safer.

    Your protocol is chosen from your AMH, AFC, age, BMI and any previous response — never a fixed clinic template. And because our protocols are aligned with ASRM (US), HFEA guidance (UK) and RANZCOG (Australia/New Zealand), your care follows the same evidence base your own doctors would work from.

    How does internal-medicine preparation improve my chances?

    A perfect embryo can still fail to implant in a body that is not physiologically ready. Before transfer we screen and stabilise the systems that quietly decide outcomes: thyroid function (TSH and anti-TPO antibodies, treated to a preconception target range), insulin resistance and PCOS (metabolic balancing that improves egg quality and lowers miscarriage risk), thrombophilia and autoimmune markers where your history warrants them, and vitamin D and micronutrients corrected well before stimulation — not during the two-week wait. Existing conditions such as diabetes, hypertension or thyroid disease are brought under control in parallel with your fertility plan.

    This is why our internal-medicine assessment runs alongside the fertility plan. A transfer scheduled into an optimised body has a materially better chance than one scheduled into an inflamed one.

    Is male-factor infertility treated properly — ICSI, PICSI and IMSI?

    It is a first-class diagnosis here, not a footnote. Alongside a full andrology work-up we use high-magnification ICSI, PICSI (physiological sperm selection by maturity) and IMSI (morphology-based selection) precisely for the cycles where standard ICSI has previously under-performed. Embryos are then grown in an ISO-standard laboratory with time-lapse culture and rapid vitrification.

    We also take the emotional side seriously: male-factor stigma is addressed directly with our dedicated psychological fertility nurse, because a diagnosis that leaves a man feeling like a bystander is a diagnosis that has not been fully treated.

    Do you offer PGT-A, and does it actually improve my odds?

    Yes. PGT-A screens embryos for chromosomal abnormalities and PGT-M tests for specific single-gene disorders. In suitable cases PGT-A can raise the live-birth rate per transfer and lower miscarriage risk, which matters especially from the mid-thirties onward.

    It is not a magic wand: PGT-A does not create embryos, and if you have very few it can leave you with nothing to transfer. We recommend it only where your age, history and embryo numbers make it genuinely worthwhile, and we say so plainly when it does not.

    I've been told my BMI or age rules me out. Will you refuse me too?

    We do not apply blanket BMI or age cut-offs of the kind that quietly close doors at home. We assess you as an individual, explain honestly what risks and realistic expectations apply at your BMI or age, and where necessary build a safer, staged plan — for example, optimising metabolic health before stimulation rather than turning you away at a number.

    Equally, we will tell you plainly when a different path is medically wiser. An honest "not yet, and here is why and what to do first" is worth more than a cycle we know is unlikely to be your best chance.

    Timeline & travel

    How long do I need to be in Cairo, and can part of the cycle be monitored at home?

    Most patients need roughly 10–14 days in Cairo for the tail-end of stimulation, egg retrieval and, in a fresh-transfer cycle, the transfer itself. A frozen-transfer cycle needs fewer days. Where your local clinic or GP is willing, the early baseline bloods and the first monitoring scans of stimulation can be done at home and shared with us through secure transfer, which shortens your stay considerably.

    The retrieval and the transfer must happen in Cairo, and we tell you precisely which visits can be remote and which cannot — before you book flights, so there are no surprises.

    What is the full timeline, from first call to pregnancy test?

    A typical fresh cycle runs about 6–8 weeks from first consultation to pregnancy test. In week one you have your remote consultation and baseline testing; stimulation runs 8–12 days with monitoring; retrieval follows; embryos are cultured to Day 5 / Day 6 blastocyst stage; then the transfer takes place. Frozen cycles are timed to your own cycle and usually complete within a similar window, with fewer days in Cairo.

    Because there is no waiting list, the pace is set by your body and your calendar — not by a queue we cannot clear.

    How do flights and travel logistics work from the US, UK, Canada, Australia or New Zealand?

    Your coordinator handles the practical side: appointment scheduling, recommended accommodation near the clinic, airport transfers, and direct-flight guidance from major US, UK, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand hubs. Cairo is served by direct flights from London, New York, Toronto and other major cities, and entry visas are typically straightforward for these nationalities. Your named person on the ground meets you with a written schedule rather than a vague promise that someone will help.

    Financial transparency

    What is the total cost, and are there any hidden medication, lab or freezing fees?

    Our packages are all-inclusive and quoted before you commit, covering consultation, medication, monitoring, retrieval, laboratory work, ICSI and — depending on the package — genetic screening and a year of embryo freezing. There are no hidden medication, lab or freezing fees added later: the quoted figure is the figure.

    The genuinely excluded items are few and always stated in writing — travel, accommodation, and any additional cycle you choose to undertake. You will never discover a medication bill or a freezing charge you were not shown up front.

    Which currencies do you quote in, and how do I pay?

    We quote transparently in USD, GBP, CAD and AUD so you can compare directly against prices at home without doing mental arithmetic at midnight. Payment is staged against clear clinical milestones and confirmed in writing, with no FX surprises buried in the small print and no deposit required to begin the conversation.

    How does your all-inclusive pricing compare with a cycle at home?

    A single private cycle in the US or Canada commonly runs US$25,000–$38,000+ once medication, genetic testing, monitoring and freezing are added line by line; in the UK, fully private IVF typically starts around £5,000–£8,000 before add-ons. Nile Smile's all-inclusive packages are positioned to deliver the same clinical scope — consultant-led IVF and ICSI in an ISO-standard laboratory — for substantially less, in your own currency, with the guarantee that the quoted figure does not quietly grow.

    Emotional care, privacy & getting started

    How does the dedicated psychological fertility nurse support both me and my husband?

    One nurse is assigned to your cycle and stays with you throughout — the same face from first injection to final debrief. She runs scheduled check-ins during stimulation, coaches you through injections and needle phobia with a written plan rather than a pep talk, and gives the two-week wait structure so you are not left alone with a search engine at midnight.

    She also schedules dedicated sessions for your partner, covering performance pressure, male-factor stigma and guilt — because in most clinics the man is informed afterwards rather than included in the decision. This emotional care is included in every package, never upsold as an extra.

    How do you protect our privacy?

    Your case is handled privately and stored securely, and no data leaves your browser on this website until you choose to send it via WhatsApp on the consultation form. Discreet scheduling, private suites and limited staff access to your file are standard, and we will not confirm to any relative that you are a patient.

    We can provide a written privacy charter on request. You are welcome to begin with a first name or an alias, and your story is never used in marketing without a separate, specific consent conversation.

    What happens if a cycle fails?

    We review every failed cycle with you properly — protocol, embryo quality, endometrial factors and timing — before deciding what changes next. Your nurse stays with you through the result, and your consultant gives you an honest, written plan for any further cycle rather than a sales call.

    We plan for a difficult result before the cycle begins: who will call you, when, and what the first week looks like. You are never left to interpret a negative test alone, and you are never asked to decide about another cycle before you are ready.

    How do I start, and what do you need from me?

    Begin with the consultation form above or a WhatsApp message. Share your age, any previous cycles, and your most recent AMH, antral follicle count or semen analysis if you have them — but do not worry if you do not. A coordinator will then arrange a private consultation with the consultant in your time zone, gather any records securely afterwards, and give you an honest answer about whether we are the right clinic for you.

    There is no deposit, no obligation, and no follow-up campaign if you ask us to stop. If we are not the right fit, we will say so and, where we can, point you somewhere better suited.
