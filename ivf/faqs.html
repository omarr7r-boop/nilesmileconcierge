01

Medical & Embryology

Will I need IVF or ICSI ? and who decides?

Your consultant decides after reviewing semen analysis, fertilisation history and egg quality. Conventional IVF lets sperm reach the egg naturally in the dish; ICSI injects a single selected sperm into each mature egg. ICSI is recommended when numbers, motility or morphology are reduced, or when previous cycles produced unexpectedly low fertilisation. The decision is written into your plan before stimulation begins, so nothing is added mid-cycle without a conversation.

How many embryos will be transferred?

For most patients under 35 with a good-quality blastocyst, one transfer is the safer and usually the wiser choice. Carrying a twin pregnancy raises the risk of prematurity, pre-eclampsia and prolonged hospital care. If you are older, have repeated failed transfers, or have few usable embryos, your consultant may discuss two. Egyptian law and clinic policy are explained to you in writing, and the final decision is made with you ? not around you.

Does PGT-A improve my chances?

PGT-A screens embryos for whole-chromosome abnormalities before transfer. It can reduce the chance of transferring an embryo that would not implant, and it lowers miscarriage risk in some age groups. It does not create healthy embryos, and it cannot guarantee a pregnancy. We offer it where the evidence supports it, explain the biopsy process, and tell you honestly when the additional cost is unlikely to change your outcome.

What happens to embryos we do not transfer?

You decide, in writing, before the cycle starts. Options include cryopreservation for a future sibling, donation for research with your consent, or disposal in line with Egyptian regulation and Sharia guidance. Storage is charged annually and the fee is shown in your quote. Embryos are never discarded, donated or used for training without a signed instruction from both partners.

02

Internal Medicine Preparation

Why does a fertility clinic ask about my blood pressure and thyroid?

Because the uterus is not a separate organ. Uncontrolled blood pressure, thyroid disease, diabetes and anaemia all affect implantation, placental development and your safety under sedation. Our internal medicine review is not a formality: it is the reason we can proceed with confidence, and occasionally the reason we delay. A short delay is always preferable to a preventable complication.

Which tests should I bring with me?

Recent full blood count, blood group and antibodies, thyroid function, vitamin D, fasting glucose or HbA1c, renal and liver function, and screening for hepatitis B, hepatitis C, HIV and syphilis. Women also need an up-to-date cervical smear, pelvic ultrasound and, where indicated, AMH. Men need a semen analysis performed within the last three months. If your results are older, we will repeat only what is clinically necessary.

Can I start treatment if I have diabetes or a heart condition?

Often yes, with preparation. We ask for a letter from your treating physician confirming that your condition is stable, your medication list, and recent monitoring results. Some drugs need adjusting before stimulation; some conditions need a cardiology or endocrine review first. We coordinate that review directly rather than asking you to arrange it alone from another country.

Do I have to stop my regular medication?

Never stop anything on your own. Most long-term medicines are continued, including many antidepressants and thyroid treatments. A small number need to be swapped or paused under supervision. Send us your complete list ? including supplements, herbal products and anything bought without a prescription ? and we will confirm the plan in writing before you travel.

03

Psychological Care Nurse

Who is the psychological fertility nurse?

She is a registered fertility nurse with additional training in emotional support and crisis communication. She is not a psychotherapist and does not replace one. Her role is to translate the medical plan into something you can live with: injection teaching, results calls, preparation for bad news, and a calm voice between appointments. You meet her before stimulation and she stays with you until the follow-up after transfer.

Is her support included in the programme fee?

Yes. The emotional intake, injection coaching, scheduled check-ins, the two-week wait support and the debrief after a negative result are all included. There is no meter running and no separate counselling invoice. If you need specialist psychotherapy, we refer you to an independent clinician and tell you the cost before you commit.

Can my husband or partner join the sessions?

Whenever you want them there. Many partners carry their own fear quietly, and a joint conversation is often the first time either of you says it aloud. We also protect space for you alone if that is what you need. You decide who is in the room, and that decision can change from one appointment to the next.

What do I do if I feel overwhelmed at two in the morning?

Message the nurse line. Non-urgent messages are answered the same working day; urgent clinical symptoms are routed to the on-call clinician. If you are in crisis, you will be given a direct number and a clear plan before you leave Cairo. We would rather receive a message that turns out to be nothing than have you sit alone with something that is not.

04

Travel & Cairo Stay

How long do I need to stay in Cairo?

Most patients plan two and a half to three weeks for a fresh cycle: monitoring, retrieval, and the days around transfer. If you are doing a frozen transfer, the stay is usually shorter. We give you a written day-by-day calendar before you book flights, and we build in a buffer for the unpredictable parts of stimulation rather than pretending they do not exist.

Can I start stimulation at home?

Often, yes. Your consultant can write a protocol that your local clinic follows for the first scans and blood tests, with results sent to us the same day. You then fly in for the final monitoring, retrieval and transfer. This works best when your local doctor is willing to communicate directly and when your travel time is short enough to be safe.

Where will I stay, and is accommodation included?

We work with a small number of vetted hotels and serviced apartments within a short drive of the clinic. The VIP Signature programme includes accommodation; Serenity does not, but we book it at the same rate we pay. You receive the supplier invoice. We do not mark up your room, and we do not put you in a property we have not inspected.

Do I need a visa or travel insurance?

Most Gulf, UK, EU, US, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand passport holders can obtain an Egyptian visa on arrival or online. Rules change, so confirm with the Egyptian embassy before you fly. Travel insurance is strongly recommended and should cover medical treatment, cancellation and repatriation. We provide the documents your insurer asks for, including the clinic address and planned dates.

Privacy & Sharia Compliance

Who can see my medical file?

Only the clinical team directly involved in your care, and only on a need-to-know basis. Your coordinator sees scheduling and contact details; embryology sees laboratory identifiers, not your name where possible. We do not confirm that you are a patient to anyone who calls ? including a relative ? unless you have named them in writing. A written privacy charter is available before you book.

Is the treatment Sharia-compliant?

Yes. Treatment is provided to married couples, using the husband's sperm and the wife's eggs, with no third-party gametes, no surrogacy and no embryo donation. Embryo handling, storage and disposal follow Egyptian regulation and the guidance of our Sharia advisory review. If a proposed test or procedure falls outside that framework, we will tell you before it is offered, not after.

What documents do we need as a married couple?

A valid marriage certificate, passports for both partners, and signed consent for treatment, embryo storage and any genetic testing. Where a document is in another language, a certified translation may be required. We send the exact list for your nationality in advance so that nothing is discovered at the clinic door.

Do you use patient stories or photographs in marketing?

Never without specific, written consent, and never in a way that identifies a patient indirectly. We do not publish success stories, baby photographs or before-and-after claims. The images on this site are licensed editorial photography, not patients. Your file, your name and your outcome are not content.

06

Transparent Pricing & Packages

What exactly is included in the programme fee?

Consultant reviews, monitoring scans and blood tests, retrieval, laboratory work, ICSI where indicated, embryo culture, transfer, and the psychological nurse support described above. Medication, genetic testing and accommodation are itemised separately so you can see what each costs. The written quote lists every line before you pay a deposit, and the deposit is tied to that quote.

Why do some prices say 'from'?

Because three variables are genuinely unknown until your consultant reviews your case: your stimulation protocol and dose, whether ICSI is needed, and how many embryos are suitable for biopsy. Once those are settled, the figure is fixed in writing. The 'from' number is an honest starting point, not a headline that quietly doubles after you arrive.

What happens if the cycle is cancelled?

The rules are set out in your signed agreement before payment. Unused laboratory and programme fees are refunded or carried forward; medication already dispensed and work already performed cannot be. We explain the exact position in writing at the point of cancellation, without pressure to rebook. Cancellation is uncommon, but it is real, and you should know the terms before you need them.

Are international patients charged more?

No. The programme fee is the same whether you live in Cairo or fly in from Riyadh, London, Toronto, Sydney or Auckland. Concierge services such as airport transfers and coordination are included. Flights, accommodation and travel insurance are billed at supplier cost with the invoice shown to you. There is no 'foreign patient' line item.
