The Patient Journey · Cairo, Egypt

Your IVF journey, from the first message to the final follow-up.

For patients in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand, IVF rarely fails for lack of medicine. It fails at the front door: a 12–24 month public waitlist, a private quote of US$25,000–$38,000+ that still grows after you commit, a BMI or age rule that closes the door before anyone has read your file, and an appointment that lasts five minutes. This page maps the alternative — week by week, what happens in Cairo, what can be done from home, and who is accountable at every stage. No waitlists, ASRM, HFEA and RANZCOG-aligned protocols, ISO-standard embryology, and transparent pricing in USD, GBP, CAD or AUD.

The timeline, honestly

Week by week, from first contact to follow-up.

Nothing here is improvised. Each stage has a purpose, an owner, and a duration we will state in writing — including the parts that can move.

  • 1

    Initial enquiry — answered within 24 hours

    You begin with the consultation form on this site or a WhatsApp message to the concierge. Tell us your age, any previous cycles, and whatever AMH, antral follicle count or semen-analysis results you already hold. A human being — not a chatbot — replies within 24 hours, usually sooner, and never pressures you to book.

  • 2

    A private video consultation in your time zone

    You meet a fertility consultant on a secure video call scheduled around your working day — New York, Toronto, London, Sydney or Auckland, it is your clock we work to. You cover your history, your goals and your budget, and you get a straight answer about whether treatment here genuinely makes sense. No deposit, no obligation, no rotating duty doctor.

  • 3

    Baseline investigation — much of it done locally

    We confirm your ovarian reserve with AMH and antral follicle count, and your partner's semen analysis, alongside routine bloods. Where it is faster or easier, these can be arranged near your home and sent to us by secure transfer; where that would only waste your time, we tell you exactly what to book and where — instead of asking you to travel first and diagnose later.

  • 4

    Your personalised protocol and written all-inclusive quote

    Your consultant designs stimulation around your own physiology — antagonist, long agonist, DuoStim or mild IVF — never a fixed clinic template, and never a blanket BMI or age refusal. You then receive a written, itemised quote in USD, GBP, CAD or AUD covering consultation, medication, monitoring, retrieval, ICSI, laboratory work and freezing. The figure you are quoted is the figure you pay.

  • 5

    Remote monitoring from home

    Early stimulation can often begin at home, with local scans and bloods shared with your Nile Smile consultant and reviewed the same day. We tell you precisely which visits can be local and which cannot — before you book a single flight. What we will not do is pretend that every stage of the cycle can be done at a distance, when it cannot.

  • 6

    Travel to Cairo

    Your coordinator sets your dates backwards from your cycle and confirms flights, transfers and accommodation near the clinic. Cairo is served by direct flights from London, New York, Toronto, Sydney and other major hubs, and entry visas are typically straightforward for US, UK, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand passport holders. You are met, not left to negotiate a taxi queue after a long flight.

  • 7

    Stimulation tail-end and trigger in Cairo

    You arrive for the closing days of stimulation, with monitoring scans every two or three days and dose adjustments made on how your body actually responds. The trigger injection is then timed to the hour — the single most time-critical moment of the whole cycle, and the precise reason this stage belongs with us in Cairo rather than split across two clinics.

  • 8

    Egg retrieval and ICSI

    Retrieval is performed under sedation in theatre, usually completed within a few hours, with the anaesthetist's name and the expected duration given to you beforehand. Mature eggs are fertilised by ICSI in our ISO-standard laboratory the same day, using high-magnification sperm selection where your history warrants it. You know the plan before you go in — not after.

  • 9

    Day-5 / Day-6 blastocyst development, with or without PGT-A

    Embryos are cultured to blastocyst stage under continuous, time-lapse monitoring. Where your age, history and embryo numbers make it worthwhile, PGT-A screens for chromosomal normality; where it would not create a usable embryo, we say so rather than sell it. You receive structured updates on development, grading, and exactly what remains for transfer or freezing.

  • 10

    Transfer or freeze-all, pregnancy test and follow-up

    A fresh transfer places a single selected embryo back in an unhurried appointment; you see the embryo image if you wish, and leave with a written plan for medication, activity and symptoms. If hyperstimulation risk or progesterone timing makes a fresh transfer unwise, all suitable embryos are vitrified for a later frozen transfer — a plan, not a failure. Then it is home, a structured two-week wait, a pregnancy test arranged locally or with us, and a results call and honest debrief wherever you are.

Planning your leave

Days in Cairo vs days at home.

Most patients need roughly 10–14 days in Cairo for a fresh cycle, and fewer for a frozen transfer. Baseline tests and early stimulation monitoring can often be done locally and shared with us securely. These are typical ranges; your own dates are confirmed in writing after your protocol is set.

At home · done remotely

  • Baseline bloods and scans — AMH, antral follicle count, semen analysis — often arranged locally
  • Early stimulation monitoring — local where your clinic cooperates, reviewed by us by secure transfer
  • The two-week wait — at home, with a structured nurse check-in schedule
  • Results and follow-up — by phone or video, wherever you are

In Cairo · Nile Smile

  • Tail-end stimulation and same-week dose adjustments
  • The trigger injection, timed to the hour
  • Egg retrieval and ICSI in an ISO-standard laboratory
  • Day-5 / Day-6 blastocyst development, and PGT-A where indicated
  • Embryo transfer — fresh or frozen — in the same accredited system
  • Roughly 10–14 days on the ground for a fresh cycle; fewer for a frozen transfer

What cannot be done remotely

  • Retrieval must be performed at the clinic — it cannot be outsourced to a local hospital
  • Transfer must also happen in Cairo, inside the same laboratory workflow that grew your embryos
  • Any cycle with high hyperstimulation risk becomes a freeze-all, which adds a later trip for transfer
  • We will never pretend that remote monitoring is the same as being in the room when it matters

Getting here

Travel logistics, handled end-to-end.

The practical side of medical travel is where most patients lose sleep — flights that clash with a scan, a hotel an hour from the clinic, medication stopped at security. Your coordinator takes all of it off your plate.

Cairo is well served for long-haul travellers. There are direct flights from London, and convenient connections from New York, Toronto, Sydney and other major US, UK, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand hubs. Entry visas are typically straightforward for these passport holders, and we advise on timing as soon as your cycle dates are set.

You do not need to organise your own transfers, guess at accommodation, or write your own medical travel letter. Each of these is confirmed with you before departure, so that when you land, the only task left is to begin.

  • Direct flights to Cairo from London, New York, Toronto, Sydney and other major hubs.
  • Entry visa guidance for US, UK, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand passport holders.
  • Airport transfers arranged and met by your coordinator on arrival.
  • Recommended accommodation near the clinic, with a direct contact who knows your dates.
  • A travel letter for airport security if you are carrying medication.
  • Every appointment, scan and call scheduled and re-scheduled for you if the plan moves.

Your coordinator handles:

  • Flights Arrival and departure dates planned backwards from your cycle, with guidance on the best hubs for direct or one-stop routes.
  • Transfers Airport pick-up on arrival and transport to and from every clinic visit within Cairo.
  • Accommodation A shortlist near the clinic, booked at your chosen budget, with a named local contact.
  • Scheduling Every scan, blood test, procedure and follow-up call arranged around your dates — and rebooked if your response changes the timeline.
  • Visa & documents Entry-visa guidance, travel letters for medication, and secure transfer of results between your local clinic and ours.

One named coordinator, reachable on WhatsApp across US, UK, EU and AU time zones — a single thread, not a call centre.

The person beside you

What your dedicated fertility nurse does at each stage.

Every Nile Smile patient is assigned one nurse who stays with the cycle from start to finish. She is not a call centre and not a chatbot with a script — she learns your history, your triggers and how you prefer to be spoken to, then keeps you steady while the medicine does its work.

Onboarding

Before any medication arrives, she holds a confidential intake call: your history, your fears, your support network, and how you want difficult news — if it comes — to be delivered.

Injection coaching

She teaches you to inject and rehearse the technique by video if you are injecting alone, and treats needle phobia as a real clinical problem with a written plan — not a pep talk.

Mid-stimulation check-ins

She checks in after your first injections, tracks how you are tolerating the medication, and answers the 11 p.m. message about a bruise, a headache or a number on a scan report.

The two-week-wait protocol

After transfer she gives the wait a structure: an agreed check-in day, guidance on what symptoms do and do not mean, and a person to call the moment the internet has frightened you.

Results debrief

A positive or a negative result is delivered by someone who already knows your story, in a scheduled call with time to sit with it — and she remains your named contact long after the airport.
