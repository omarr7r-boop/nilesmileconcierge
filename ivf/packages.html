    Why we price this way

    The bill should never be the second shock.

    IVF pricing is usually broken into fragments: a headline cycle fee, then medication, anaesthesia, laboratory work, freezing, storage and follow-up, each appearing when you are too invested to argue. The final number can be forty per cent above the one you were quoted.

    We do not work that way. Your programme is quoted as a single, itemised total. Every inclusion is listed. Every exclusion is listed too, in the same document, so you can see the boundary of what you are buying.

    Medication is the one genuinely variable line, because the dose depends on your protocol and your response. It appears in your quote with the brand and the planned dose, and it is locked before stimulation begins. If your consultant changes the plan for a clinical reason, the change is explained and agreed with you before it is charged.

    • No payment is taken before you have read and approved the full quote.
    • Optional treatments are labelled optional, with the consequences of declining explained.
    • If the final invoice differs from the signed quote without your written agreement, we honour the quote.

    Included in every programme

    • Your named lead consultant, start to finish.
    • Baseline, monitoring and pre-transfer scans.
    • Blood tests required to run the cycle safely.
    • Anaesthesia and theatre fees for retrieval.
    • ICSI, embryology and blastocyst culture.
    • A fresh embryo transfer, where clinically appropriate.
    • Your named coordinator and dedicated fertility nurse.
    • Emotional intake, injection coaching and results debrief.
    • A written summary for your doctor at home.

    Not a marketing list. These are the line items that appear in your quote.

    Questions international patients ask us

    Everything you want to know, answered honestly.

    Pricing & inclusions

    What is the exact total cost of a Nile Smile cycle, and what is genuinely excluded?

    Serenity starts from US$2,500 and VIP Signature from US$4,200, and both figures are all-inclusive of the clinical cycle: your named consultant, medication, monitoring scans and bloods, egg retrieval with anaesthesia, high-magnification ICSI, blastocyst culture, laboratory work and a fresh transfer — plus your dedicated psychological fertility nurse. VIP Signature adds PGT-A for up to five embryos and one year of embryo freezing.

    The final figure is confirmed in a written, itemised quote after your consultation and before any payment is taken. The only genuine extras are international flights, visa and travel insurance, accommodation and personal expenses, and any additional self-chosen cycle or frozen transfer beyond the plan. For comparison, a single private cycle in the US or Canada commonly runs US$25,000–$38,000+ once medication, genetic testing and freezing are added.

    IVF prices are usually a moving target. How can you guarantee there are no hidden fees?

    Because the guarantee is a written clause, not a slogan. The quote you approve is the price you pay: if the final invoice differs from the signed quote without your written agreement, we honour the quote. Every exclusion is listed in the same document as the price, before you pay anything, and nothing optional is added silently.

    Medication — the one genuinely variable line in most clinics — is itemised with the brand and the planned dose and locked before stimulation begins. The only time it changes is when your consultant adjusts the protocol for a clinical reason, and that change is explained and agreed with you before it is charged. You will never receive an unexpected medication, laboratory or freezing invoice after the cycle.

    Which currencies do you quote in, and how do I pay?

    We quote transparently in USD, and by equivalent in GBP, CAD and AUD, so you can compare our all-inclusive figure directly with prices in your own country without guessing at exchange rates. Payment is staged against clear milestones — a booking deposit to confirm your dates and laboratory slot, then the balance before stimulation — and every payment is receipted with a description of what it covers.

    Accepted payment methods and the exact schedule are set out in your signed agreement, with no FX surprises buried in the small print. Instalment arrangements are sometimes possible depending on your booking date; ask your coordinator directly and we will tell you honestly what is achievable.

    How does your all-inclusive price compare with a cycle at home?

    In the US and Canada, a single private cycle typically runs US$25,000–$38,000+ once medication, genetic testing, monitoring and freezing are itemised. In the UK, fully private IVF often starts around £5,000–£8,000 before add-ons that can double it. In Australia and New Zealand, private cycles frequently leave patients A$9,000–A$15,000+ out of pocket once the limited public rebate is applied — and public pathways carry 12–24 month waits.

    Nile Smile's all-inclusive packages are positioned to deliver the same clinical scope — consultant-led IVF, ICSI, an ISO-standard embryology laboratory and genetic screening — for substantially less, with the guarantee that the quoted figure is the figure. You are not trading clinical quality for price; you are removing the waitlist, the gatekeeping and the bill shock.

    Clinical protocols & success

    What are realistic success rates, and how much does age really matter?

    Success depends far more on age and ovarian reserve than on the clinic. As a general guide for a single transfer of a genetically normal Day-5 blastocyst, live-birth rates are roughly 50–60% under 35, 40–50% at 35–37, 30–40% at 38–40, and progressively lower above 40. With PGT-A screening and a euploid embryo, the outcome per transfer improves — but the number of usable embryos available to you falls with age.

    That is why we give you a personalised estimate after your AMH, antral follicle count and semen analysis, rather than a brochure average. No clinic can promise a baby, and any that does is selling rather than treating. Our role is to give every cycle its fairest possible chance.

    How does internal-medicine preparation change my odds?

    An embryo can be perfect and still fail to implant in a body that is not physiologically ready. Before transfer we screen and stabilise the systems that quietly decide outcomes: thyroid function (TSH and anti-TPO antibodies, treated to a preconception target), insulin resistance and PCOS, thrombophilia and autoimmune markers where your history warrants it, and vitamin D and micronutrient levels — all corrected well before stimulation, not during the two-week wait.

    Chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension and thyroid disease are brought under control in coordination with your existing specialists. A transfer scheduled into an optimised body has a materially better chance than one scheduled into an inflamed or unbalanced one, which is why our medical assessment runs in parallel with, not after, the fertility plan.

    Is male-factor infertility treated properly, or as an afterthought?

    It is a first-class diagnosis here. Every cycle includes a full andrology work-up, and where standard ICSI has previously under-performed we use high-magnification ICSI, PICSI (physiological sperm selection) and IMSI (morphology-based selection) — selecting sperm by maturity and structure rather than movement alone. Time-lapse incubation lets us assess embryo development without disturbing the culture.

    Male-factor diagnosis also carries a silent emotional weight that many clinics ignore. Our dedicated nurse runs partner sessions that name the performance pressure, stigma and guilt directly, so the man in the room is an active partner in the decision rather than a bystander to it.

    Which stimulation protocols do you use, and why does ASRM/HFEA/RANZCOG alignment matter?

    We use the full evidence-based range: antagonist (flexible and the standard for most cycles), long agonist for specific endometriosis, adenomyosis or synchronisation needs, DuoStim for urgent or low-reserve cases, and mild/Mini-IVF for low responders or where OHSS safety is a concern. Your protocol is chosen from your AMH, antral follicle count, age, BMI and prior response — not a fixed clinic template.

    Our clinical protocols are aligned with the American Society for Reproductive Medicine (ASRM), UK HFEA guidance and the Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (RANZCOG) — the same evidence base your own doctors work from. If your treatment is in Cairo, the medicine is still held to the standard you already trust at home.

    Timeline & travel

    How many days do I actually need in Cairo, and can I monitor remotely?

    Most patients need roughly 10–14 days in Cairo for the stimulation tail-end, egg retrieval and, in a fresh transfer cycle, the transfer itself. A frozen-transfer cycle needs fewer days. Where your local clinic is willing, the baseline bloods and scans, and the early monitoring scans of stimulation, can frequently be done near home and shared with your Nile Smile consultant by secure transfer — shortening your stay.

    Egg retrieval and transfer must happen in Cairo. We tell you precisely which visits can be remote and which cannot, in writing, before you book flights — so your itinerary is built on certainty, not hope.

    What is the full timeline, from first call to pregnancy test?

    A typical fresh cycle runs around 6–8 weeks from first consultation to pregnancy test: a remote consultation and baseline testing in week one, stimulation for 8–12 days (much of which can be monitored locally), egg retrieval, then Day-5/6 blastocyst development and transfer. The pregnancy test follows roughly 10–14 days after transfer.

    Frozen cycles are timed to your own menstrual cycle and usually complete within a similar window, with fewer days in Cairo. Once your protocol is set, your coordinator sends a written day-by-day schedule so you know exactly which days you must be in Egypt and which you can spend at home.

    How does travel and accommodation coordination work — are there direct flights?

    Your coordinator handles the practical side: appointment scheduling, recommended accommodation near the clinic at partner rates, airport transfers, and direct-flight guidance from major US, UK, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand hubs. Cairo is served by direct flights from London, New York, Toronto and other major cities, and entry visas are typically straightforward for these nationals.

    If we arrange travel or accommodation for you, it is billed at cost with the supplier's invoice shown to you — we do not take a markup on your flights or your hotel. VIP Signature includes a private chauffeur for every clinic visit and priority scheduling built around your travel dates, including a private arrival appointment.

    What happens if my dates change, or the cycle is cancelled?

    The rules are set out in your signed agreement before you pay, so there are no surprises. Broadly: unused laboratory and programme fees are refunded or carried forward, while medication already dispensed and work already performed cannot be recovered. We explain the exact position at the point of cancellation, in writing, with no pressure to rebook.

    Because a cancelled or delayed cycle is emotionally and financially stressful, we also recommend travel insurance that covers medical disruption. If the numbers or the timing no longer work, tell us — sometimes a staged plan, a simpler protocol or a later start is the honest answer, and we would rather adjust the plan than begin a cycle you cannot comfortably finish.

    Privacy, emotional care & getting started

    Who is the dedicated psychological fertility nurse, and what does she actually do?

    One nurse is assigned to your cycle and stays with you throughout — she is not a call centre, and she is not a chatbot with a script. She learns your history, your triggers and the way you prefer to be spoken to. During stimulation she runs scheduled check-ins and coaches you through injections or needle phobia with a written plan rather than a pep talk. Through the two-week wait she provides structure, symptom guidance and a person to call when the internet has frightened you at midnight.

    She also holds dedicated sessions for your partner, addressing performance pressure, male-factor stigma and guilt, and she is the person who delivers a difficult result with the time it deserves — because she already knows your story. This nurse is included in every package, from first injection to final debrief. It is never an upsell.

    How do you protect our privacy?

    Your case is handled privately and stored securely, and no data leaves your browser on this website until you choose to send it via the consultation form or WhatsApp. Scheduling is discreet, consultations take place in private suites, and staff access to your file is limited to those directly involved in your care. A written privacy charter is available on request.

    We understand that fertility treatment can carry cultural and personal sensitivities, and we take the confidentiality of an international patient's treatment seriously as part of a first-class standard of care.

    What happens if the cycle fails?

    We review every failed cycle with you properly — protocol, embryo quality, endometrial factors and transfer timing — before deciding what changes next, rather than repeating the same approach and hoping. Your consultant gives you an honest, written plan for any further cycle, not a sales call, and your nurse stays with you through the result itself.

    IVF is not a guarantee, and anyone who promises a baby is selling rather than treating. What we can promise is that you will never be left to interpret a negative test alone, and that any decision about continuing, pausing or stopping is made with you, in your own time.

    How do I start, and what do you need from me?

    Begin with the consultation form or a WhatsApp message. Share your age, any prior cycles, and your most recent AMH, antral follicle count or semen analysis if you have them — plus any earlier IVF records, if any. You do not need everything to start; it simply shortens the path to a personalised plan.

    A coordinator then arranges a private consultation with your consultant in your time zone. There is no deposit and no obligation, and we will give you an honest answer about whether we are the right clinic for you — including recommending a different path if that is genuinely wiser.
