Why we price this way

The bill should never be the second shock.

IVF pricing is usually broken into fragments: a headline cycle fee, then medication, anaesthesia, laboratory work, freezing, storage and follow-up, each appearing when you are too invested to argue. The final number can be forty per cent above the one you were quoted.

We do not work that way. Your programme is quoted as a single, itemised total. Every inclusion is listed. Every exclusion is listed too, in the same document, so you can see the boundary of what you are buying.

Medication is the one genuinely variable line, because the dose depends on your protocol and your response. It appears in your quote with the brand and the planned dose, and it is locked before stimulation begins. If your consultant changes the plan for a clinical reason, the change is explained and agreed with you before it is charged.

  • No payment is taken before you have read and approved the full quote.
  • Optional treatments are labelled optional, with the consequences of declining explained.
  • If the final invoice differs from the signed quote without your written agreement, we honour the quote.

Included in every programme

  • Your named lead consultant, start to finish.
  • Baseline, monitoring and pre-transfer scans.
  • Blood tests required to run the cycle safely.
  • Anaesthesia and theatre fees for retrieval.
  • ICSI, embryology and blastocyst culture.
  • A fresh embryo transfer, where clinically appropriate.
  • Your named coordinator and dedicated fertility nurse.

The zero-hidden-cost guarantee

Five promises, in writing.

This is not a marketing slogan. Each promise appears in your signed programme agreement, and each one has a named person accountable for it.

  • The quote you approve is the price you pay. If the final invoice differs without your written agreement, we honour the quote.
  • Every exclusion is listed before you pay. Flights, accommodation and anything outside the programme appear in the same document, not in a later email.
  • Nothing optional is added silently. If a treatment is not clinically indicated, we will not bill for it — and we will tell you why we are declining.
  • No payment before the medical plan is approved. You will never be asked to transfer money to find out what the plan is.
  • A written receipt for every payment, with a clear description of what it covers and what remains.

The honest part

What is not included — stated plainly.

A programme that claims to include everything is either overpriced or hiding something. These are the costs that sit outside both programmes. We list them here because discovering them later is exactly the experience we built Nile Smile to avoid.

If you want us to arrange any of them, we will — at cost, with the supplier’s invoice shown to you. We do not take a markup on your flights or your hotel.

Outside the programme fee

  • International flights, visa fees and travel insurance.
  • Accommodation, meals and personal expenses.
  • Medication beyond the protocol in your quote, if your response requires a change.
  • PGT-M probe design and genetic counselling for a known inherited condition.
  • Embryo storage beyond the first twelve months.
  • Additional cycles, frozen transfers or surgical procedures not in the plan.
  • Treatment for complications requiring hospital admission, which is billed by the hospital.

If any of these becomes relevant, we tell you the cost before it is arranged — never after.

Payment and commitment

You should know exactly what you are committing to.

Fertility treatment is a significant financial decision, often made under pressure. We will never use that pressure to hurry you. Take the quote home. Show it to your partner, your family, or a financial advisor. Ask us to explain any line you do not understand.

Once you are ready, a booking deposit confirms your dates and your place in the laboratory schedule. The balance is due before stimulation begins, in line with the schedule in your signed agreement. Every payment is receipted.

If the numbers do not work, tell us. Sometimes a staged plan, a simpler protocol or a later start is the honest answer. We would rather lose a booking than begin a cycle you cannot afford to finish.

Money questions

The questions people are embarrassed to ask.

Ask them anyway. A clinic that flinches at a pricing question is telling you something.

Is medication really included?

Yes — it appears as an itemised line in your quote, with the brand and planned dose. The only time it changes is if your consultant adjusts your protocol for a clinical reason, and that change is explained and agreed before it is charged. You will never receive an unexpected medication invoice after the cycle.

Why is the price “from” rather than fixed?

Because the honest variables are real: your protocol, your medication dose, whether ICSI is indicated and how many embryos are suitable for genetic testing. Once those are known, the number is fixed and written down. The “from” figure is an indicative starting point, not a bait price that doubles later.

What happens to my money if the cycle is cancelled?

It depends on the stage, and the rules are set out in your signed agreement before you pay. Broadly: unused laboratory and programme fees are refunded or carried forward, while medication already dispensed and work already performed cannot be. We explain the exact position at the point of cancellation, in writing, with no pressure to rebook.

Can I pay in instalments?

Sometimes. It depends on the schedule and the point at which you book. Ask your coordinator directly — we will tell you honestly what is possible rather than promising flexibility we cannot deliver. What we will not do is encourage you to borrow beyond what is safe for your family.

Do you charge more because I am an international patient?

No. The programme fee is the same whether you live in Cairo or fly in from Riyadh, London or Lagos. Concierge services such as transfers are included. Flights, accommodation and travel insurance are billed at cost, with the supplier’s invoice shown to you.

Can I upgrade from Serenity to VIP Signature later?

Usually yes, if the laboratory schedule allows and the change happens before the relevant stage — for example, before embryos are biopsied. You will receive a revised written quote showing exactly what changes. We will not upgrade you silently or bill you for something you did not approve.
