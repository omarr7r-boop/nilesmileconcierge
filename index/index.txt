<section id="faq" class="section bg-white reveal">
  <div class="container-narrow">
    <div class="text-center mb-12">
      <p class="eyebrow mb-4">Frequently Asked</p>
      <h2 class="text-nile-blue mb-4" style="font-size:clamp(1.75rem,4vw,2.5rem)">Your Questions, Answered</h2>
    </div>

    <details>
      <summary>Is Egypt safe for medical travel?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">Yes. Egypt welcomed over 19 million tourists in 2025 — a record year. Our partner clinics are located in secure, upscale districts of Cairo. We provide private VIP transportation throughout your stay and a dedicated coordinator accompanies you to all clinic visits.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>Do I need a visa to enter Egypt?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">US, Canadian, UK, EU, Australian, and New Zealand passport holders can obtain a visa on arrival at Egyptian airports for $25 USD (valid 30 days). We provide full documentation to support your entry, including an invitation letter and treatment confirmation.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>Are your dentists licensed and certified?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">Yes. All treating dentists are licensed by the Egyptian Dental Syndicate and hold postgraduate qualifications from top institutions (Cairo, Alexandria, Ain Shams). Credentials are shared with you before booking.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What happens if I have a problem after returning home?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">You receive a written aftercare protocol, scheduled telehealth follow-ups (1 week, 1 month, 3 months), and a documented lifetime warranty on implants. We coordinate with a licensed local dentist in your country when needed.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What implant brands do you use?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">We work exclusively with Straumann (Switzerland) and Nobel Biocare (Sweden/USA) — the two highest-ranked implant systems in clinical studies for esthetic outcomes and long-term success. Osstem (South Korea) is available as a cost-effective alternative for suitable cases.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>Can I combine treatment with a family vacation?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">Absolutely. Many patients bring their families. While you are in treatment or recovery, your family can enjoy curated tours of the Pyramids, Nile cruises, Red Sea resorts, and the Egyptian Museum. We arrange family-friendly accommodation.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>Does my insurance cover treatment in Egypt?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">In most cases, US, Canadian, and UK dental insurance does not cover treatment abroad. Treat all pricing as self-pay unless you have written confirmation from your insurer. We provide itemized invoices for you to submit.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What are the risks of dental implants?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">Implant success rates exceed 94.6% in systematic reviews. The most common complication is peri-implantitis (inflammation around the implant), affecting approximately 9.6% of implants. Our prevention protocol includes CBCT-guided planning, premium implant surfaces, and 6-month maintenance intervals. Full written disclosure of risks is provided before treatment.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What if I need a second visit?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">Complex cases — such as those requiring extensive bone grafting or sinus lifts — may require a healing phase of 4–6 months before final implant placement. If multiple visits are necessary, this is clearly outlined and priced in your initial written treatment plan.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>Can I pay in installments?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">We operate on a structured milestone payment system (Holding Deposit, Protected Retainer, Balance at Clinic). While we do not offer direct in-house financing, many of our North American patients utilize third-party medical loans to cover their care.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What languages do you speak?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">Our entire concierge team, medical coordinators, and partner dentists are fully fluent in English and Arabic. Translation services for other languages (French, Spanish, German) can be arranged upon request prior to your arrival.</div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What anesthesia options are available?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">
        <p class="mb-3">We offer three levels of anesthesia, selected based on your procedure complexity and personal preference:</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>1. Local Anesthesia</strong> — Standard for single implants, crowns, and minor procedures. You remain fully awake and comfortable, with no pain sensation in the treated area.</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>2. Local Anesthesia + Oral Sedation</strong> — For patients with dental anxiety. Oral sedatives (like Valium or Halcion) are taken 30–60 minutes before the procedure. You remain conscious but deeply relaxed.</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>3. IV Sedation (Conscious Sedation)</strong> — Administered by a licensed anesthesiologist. You're in a dream-like state, relaxed and comfortable, with no memory of the procedure. Ideal for full-arch (All-on-4 / All-on-6) surgeries.</p>
        <p class="mt-3"><strong>Important:</strong> General anesthesia is not typically required for dental implant procedures. All anesthesia is administered or supervised by licensed medical professionals in our JCI-accredited partner clinics.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What implant systems and brands do you offer?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">
        <p class="mb-3">We work with the world's leading implant systems. Here's a full comparison:</p>
        <div class="space-y-3 mt-4">
          <div class="bg-nile-beige/50 p-3 border-l-2 border-nile-gold">
            <p class="font-semibold text-nile-blue mb-1">Straumann (Switzerland) — Premium</p>
            <p class="text-xs text-nile-muted">Highest success rate (99.2% at 10 years). Best for esthetic zones and complex cases. Bone Level & Tissue Level systems. Cost: from $1,200/implant</p>
          </div>
          <div class="bg-nile-beige/50 p-3 border-l-2 border-nile-gold">
            <p class="font-semibold text-nile-blue mb-1">Nobel Biocare (Sweden/USA) — Premium</p>
            <p class="text-xs text-nile-muted">Pioneer of modern implantology. TiUnite surface promotes rapid healing. NobelActive & NobelParallel systems. Cost: from $1,100/implant</p>
          </div>
          <div class="bg-nile-beige/50 p-3 border-l-2 border-nile-gold">
            <p class="font-semibold text-nile-blue mb-1">Osstem (South Korea) — Value</p>
            <p class="text-xs text-nile-muted">Excellent cost-to-quality ratio. Widely used in Asia and Europe. Suitable for standard cases. Cost: from $600/implant</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <p class="mt-3 text-xs italic">Your treating dentist will recommend the optimal system for your specific case during your free clinical review.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>Who is a good candidate for dental implants?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">
        <p class="mb-3">Good candidates for dental implants generally meet these criteria:</p>
        <ul class="space-y-2 list-disc pl-5">
          <li>Adults over 18 (jawbone must be fully developed)</li>
          <li>Adequate bone density — or willing to undergo bone grafting</li>
          <li>Healthy gum tissue with no active periodontal disease</li>
          <li>No uncontrolled chronic conditions (diabetes, heart disease)</li>
          <li>Non-smoker, or willing to quit during healing</li>
          <li>Committed to good oral hygiene and regular follow-ups</li>
          <li>Able to travel to Cairo for treatment (7–14 days minimum)</li>
        </ul>
        <p class="mt-3"><strong>Not suitable for:</strong> Patients on IV bisphosphonates for cancer, those with recent heart attack (within 6 months), active cancer treatment, uncontrolled diabetes, or severe immune disorders.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What medications will I need after surgery?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">
        <p class="mb-3">Your dentist will prescribe a personalized medication plan. Typical protocols include:</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>Antibiotics:</strong> Amoxicillin (or Clindamycin for penicillin-allergic patients) — usually 5–7 days post-op to prevent infection.</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>Pain relief:</strong> Ibuprofen 400mg (every 6–8 hours) and/or Paracetamol 500mg. Avoid aspirin, which can increase bleeding.</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>Anti-inflammatory:</strong> Prescribed for swelling control during the first 3–5 days.</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>Antiseptic mouthwash:</strong> Chlorhexidine 0.12% — starting 24 hours after surgery.</p>
        <p class="mt-3"><strong>Important:</strong> Never exceed the maximum daily dose of Paracetamol (4,000 mg per day for adults). Do not combine multiple medications containing Paracetamol without medical advice.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>What does the aftercare protocol include?<span class="acc-icon">+</span></summary>
      <div class="acc-body">
        <p class="mb-3">You receive a complete written aftercare protocol before departure, covering:</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>Day 1–3:</strong> Rest, ice packs (20 min on / 20 min off), soft cold foods, avoid rinsing forcefully, no smoking, no straws.</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>Day 4–7:</strong> Gentle warm salt water rinses, soft foods (yogurt, soup, mashed potatoes), start prescribed antibiotics and mouthwash.</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>Week 2–4:</strong> Return to most normal activities, gradually introduce semi-solid foods, avoid hard/crunchy items, hygiene checks via telehealth.</p>
        <p class="mb-2"><strong>Long-term:</strong> Brush twice daily with a soft brush, use interdental brushes and water flossers, attend 6-month check-ups (in Cairo or with a local dentist we coordinate with).</p>
        <p class="mt-3">You'll also receive emergency contact information and 24/7 WhatsApp access to your clinical coordinator.</p>
      </div>
    </details>

    const startY = window.pageYOffset;
    const distance = targetY - startY;
    const absDistance = Math.abs(distance);
    
    // ⚡ مدة أطول = cinematic
    const duration = Math.min(1400, Math.max(700, absDistance * 0.7));
    let startTs = null;
    
    // easeInOutExpo — الأنعم على الإطلاق (framer-motion style)
    const ease = t => {
      if (t === 0) return 0;
      if (t === 1) return 1;
      return t < 0.5
        ? Math.pow(2, 20 * t - 10) / 2
        : (2 - Math.pow(2, -20 * t + 10)) / 2;
    };
    
    function step(ts){
      if(!startTs) startTs = ts;
      const p = Math.min((ts - startTs) / duration, 1);
      window.scrollTo(0, startY + distance * ease(p));
      if(p < 1) requestAnimationFrame(step);
    }
    requestAnimationFrame(step);
    
    if(history.pushState) history.pushState(null,'',href);
  });
});

  /* ═════════ 7. GOLD PARTICLES ═════════ */
  if(window.innerWidth > 768 && !prefersReduced){
    const frag = document.createDocumentFragment();
    const particleCount = window.innerWidth > 1024 ? 12 : 6;
for(let i=0;i<particleCount;i++){
      const p = document.createElement('div');
      p.className = 'particle';
      p.style.left = (5 + Math.random()*90) + '%';
      p.style.animationDelay = (Math.random()*14) + 's';
      p.style.animationDuration = (11 + Math.random()*7) + 's';
      frag.appendChild(p);
    }
    document.body.appendChild(frag);
  }

  /* ═════════ 8. HERO PARALLAX (subtle) ═════════ */
  const heroImg = document.getElementById('heroImg');
  if(heroImg && !prefersReduced && window.innerWidth > 768){
    let heroTick = false;
    window.addEventListener('scroll', ()=>{
      if(heroTick) return;
      heroTick = true;
      requestAnimationFrame(()=>{
        const y = window.scrollY;
        if(y < window.innerHeight){
          heroImg.style.transform = 'translateY(' + (y * 0.22) + 'px) scale(1.04)';
        }
        heroTick = false;
      });
    }, {passive:true});
  }

  /* ═════════ 9. MAGNETIC BUTTONS ═════════ */
  if(window.innerWidth > 1024 && !prefersReduced){
    document.querySelectorAll('.btn-gold').forEach(btn=>{
      btn.addEventListener('mousemove', (e)=>{
        const r = btn.getBoundingClientRect();
        const x = e.clientX - r.left - r.width/2;
        const y = e.clientY - r.top - r.height/2;
        btn.style.transform = `translate(${x*0.10}px, ${y*0.10}px) translateY(-2px)`;
      });
      btn.addEventListener('mouseleave', ()=>{ btn.style.transform = ''; });
    });
  }

  /* ═════════ 10. MODALS ═════════ */
  let lastFocused = null;
  window.openModal = function(id){
    const m = document.getElementById(id);
    if(!m) return;
    lastFocused = document.activeElement;
    m.classList.add('open');
    document.body.style.overflow = 'hidden';
    const focusables = m.querySelectorAll('button, [href], input, select, textarea, [tabindex]:not([tabindex="-1"])');
    if (focusables.length) setTimeout(() => focusables[0].focus(), 60);
  };
  window.closeModal = function(id){
    const m = document.getElementById(id);
    if(!m) return;
    m.classList.remove('open');
    document.body.style.overflow = '';
    if (lastFocused && typeof lastFocused.focus === 'function') lastFocused.focus();
  };

  // Focus trap — يمنع الـTab يخرج من الـmodal المفتوح
  document.addEventListener('keydown', e => {
    if (e.key !== 'Tab') return;
    const open = document.querySelector('.modal.open');
    if (!open) return;
    const focusables = Array.from(open.querySelectorAll('button, [href], input, select, textarea, [tabindex]:not([tabindex="-1"])'))
      .filter(el => !el.disabled && el.offsetParent !== null);
    if (!focusables.length) return;
    const first = focusables[0];
    const last = focusables[focusables.length - 1];
    if (e.shiftKey && document.activeElement === first) {
      e.preventDefault();
      last.focus();
    } else if (!e.shiftKey && document.activeElement === last) {
      e.preventDefault();
      first.focus();
    }
  });
  document.querySelectorAll('.modal').forEach(m=>{
    m.addEventListener('click', e=>{ if(e.target === m) window.closeModal(m.id); });
  });
  document.addEventListener('keydown', e=>{
    if(e.key === 'Escape'){
      document.querySelectorAll('.modal.open').forEach(m=>window.closeModal(m.id));
    }
  });

  /* ═════════ 11. LEAD FORM ═════════ */
  const leadForm = document.getElementById('leadForm');
  if(leadForm){
    leadForm.addEventListener('submit', e=>{
      e.preventDefault();
      const email = (leadForm.querySelector('input[type="email"]') || {}).value || '';
      const msg = encodeURIComponent(`Hi Nile Smile, I'd like a free X-ray review. My email: ${email}`);
      window.open('https://wa.me/201503308333?text=' + msg, '_blank', 'noopener');
    });
  }

  /* ═════════ 12. AUTO-ENHANCE ═════════ */
  document.querySelectorAll('.logo-wrap').forEach(wrap => {
    if (!wrap.querySelector('.signature-line')) {
      const line = document.createElement('span');
      line.className = 'signature-line';
      line.setAttribute('aria-hidden','true');
      wrap.appendChild(line);
    }
  });
 document.querySelectorAll('.btn-gold').forEach(btn => {
    const text = (btn.textContent || '').toLowerCase().trim();
    
    // كل الأزرار الذهبية تاخد shine-auto
    btn.classList.add('shine-auto');
    
    // الأزرار الرئيسية تاخد breathe كمان
    if (text.includes('consultation') || 
        text.includes('book free') || 
        text.includes('treatment plan') ||
        text.includes('send my')) {
      btn.classList.add('breathe');
    }
  });
   document.querySelectorAll('section h2, footer h2').forEach(h2 => {
    const section = h2.closest('section, footer');
    if (!section) return;
    
    // لو القسم داكن → جراديينت ذهبي
    const isDark = section.classList.contains('bg-nile-blue') ||
                   section.classList.contains('bg-nile-blue-dark') ||
                   section.id === 'science' ||
                   section.id === 'consult' ||
                   section.tagName === 'FOOTER';
    
    h2.classList.add(isDark ? 'gold-grad' : 'section-heading');
  });


  /* ═════════ 13. COOKIE BANNER ═════════ */
  if (!localStorage.getItem('cookiesAccepted')) {
    setTimeout(() => {
      const banner = document.getElementById('cookieBanner');
      if (banner) {
        banner.classList.remove('hidden');
        // ارفع الـbody فوق عشان الـcookie banner ميغطيش الـsticky bar
        if (window.innerWidth < 768) document.body.style.paddingBottom = '160px';
      }
    }, 2500);
  }

  /* ═════════ 14. EXIT-INTENT POPUP ═════════ */
  let exitShown = false;
  document.addEventListener('mouseleave', (e) => {
    if (e.clientY < 0 && !exitShown && !sessionStorage.getItem('exitShown')) {
      const popup = document.getElementById('exitPopup');
      if (popup) {
        popup.classList.remove('hidden');
        popup.classList.add('flex');
        exitShown = true;
        sessionStorage.setItem('exitShown', 'true');
      }
    }
  });
  window.closeExitPopup = function() {
    const popup = document.getElementById('exitPopup');
    if (popup) { popup.classList.add('hidden'); popup.classList.remove('flex'); }
  };

  /* ═════════ 15. TRUST SIGNAL (Truthful — no fake data) ═════════ */
  setTimeout(() => {
    const n = document.getElementById('activity-notification');
    if (n) {
      n.classList.remove('hidden');
      setTimeout(() => n.classList.add('hidden'), 9000);
    }
  }, 15000);
  /* ═════════ 16. SCROLL BUTTON FEEDBACK ═════════ */
  document.querySelectorAll('a[href^="#"]').forEach(btn => {
    btn.addEventListener('click', function() {
      // تأثير بصري عند الضغط
      this.style.transform = 'scale(0.96)';
      setTimeout(() => {
        this.style.transform = '';
      }, 180);
    });
  });
})();
</script>

/* ═════════ STICKY TOC — Highlight current section ═════════ */
(function(){
  const toc = document.getElementById('stickyToc');
  const dots = toc.querySelectorAll('.toc-dot');
  const sections = Array.from(dots).map(d => document.querySelector(d.getAttribute('href'))).filter(Boolean);
  
  function updateToc(){
    const scrollY = window.scrollY;
    if(scrollY > 600) toc.classList.add('show');
    else toc.classList.remove('show');
    
    let current = null;
    sections.forEach(sec => {
      const top = sec.offsetTop - 200;
      if(scrollY >= top) current = sec.id;
    });
    
    dots.forEach(d => {
      const isActive = d.getAttribute('href') === '#' + current;
      d.classList.toggle('active', isActive);
    });
  }
  
  window.addEventListener('scroll', updateToc, {passive:true});
  updateToc();
})();
